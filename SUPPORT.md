# Getting help

- To understand an error, read [docs/troubleshooting.md](docs/troubleshooting.md)
  first. Most errors also print a `Fix:` line.
- To report a bug, ask for a feature or ask a question, open an
  [issue](https://github.com/pilef-co/bqto/issues/new/choose).
- To report a vulnerability, do not open an issue. Follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- For bqto Pro, join the beta at [bqto.dev](https://bqto.dev/#waitlist).

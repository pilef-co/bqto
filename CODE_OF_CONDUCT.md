# Code of conduct

bqto follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the issues and pull requests of this repository, and anywhere
you represent the project.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep criticism about the work,
not the person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

To report a problem, email pedro@pilef.co. Only the maintainers read the
report, and they keep the name of the person who reports it private.

The maintainers decide what to do, from a private correction to a permanent
ban, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
